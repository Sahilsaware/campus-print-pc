Privacy Policy & Data Security

Campus Print is built so that your documents stay yours. Here is exactly what happens to your file.

1. No account, no personal data

You do not need to log in. We do not ask for your name, phone number or e-mail.

2. Your document is deleted right after printing

  • Your file is stored on our server only until it has been printed.
  • As soon as the printer finishes, the document is deleted automatically.
  • If you upload a file but never pay, it is deleted within about one hour.
  • If a print fails, the file is kept for at most 24 hours so the print desk can retry or refund it, then it is deleted.

3. Secure transfer

  • Files travel over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.
  • The print computer connects to our server with a secret key. Nobody else can download your file.

4. Payments

Online payments are handled by Razorpay. We never see or store your card, UPI or bank details. We only keep the order amount and the payment reference needed for refunds.

5. What we keep

After printing we keep only the file name, page count, print settings, amount and time. This is used for the print desk's daily accounts and never contains the content of your document.

6. Questions

Ask the print desk. Please keep your Job ID (shown on the print status screen) ready.
